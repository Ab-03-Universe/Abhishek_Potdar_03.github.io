About

I am an Astronomy and Astrophysics researcher working primarily in radio astronomy, solar radio physics, and radio instrumentation. My current research focuses on understanding the temporal association between solar flares and radio bursts, particularly Type II and Type III bursts. I am also involved in development of radio astronomy instrumentation including feed systems, SDR-based receivers, bandpass filters, and automated telescope tracking systems.

Research Interests

Solar Radio Bursts

Study of Type II and Type III radio bursts, dynamic spectra, and burst energetics.

Solar Flare Physics

Temporal association between X-ray flares, CMEs, and radio emissions.

Radio Instrumentation

Development of feedhorns, SDR receivers, and RF systems for radio astronomy.

21-cm HI Observations

Neutral hydrogen observations using automated radio telescope systems.

Research Projects

3-Meter Automated Radio Telescope

Design and development of an automated 3-meter radio telescope operating at 1.42 GHz for neutral hydrogen observations.

Solar Radio Burst Analysis

Statistical analysis of solar flare timing and radio burst associations using multi-instrument observations.

Feedhorn & RF System Design

HFSS simulation and optimization of feedhorns, choke-ring structures, filters, and RF signal chains.
