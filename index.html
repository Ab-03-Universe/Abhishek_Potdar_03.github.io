Abhishek Potdar

Solar Radio Astrophysics | Radio Astronomy Instrumentation | Space Weather

I am an early-career astrophysics researcher working in Solar Radio Physics, Radio Astronomy Instrumentation, and High-Energy Solar Observations. My research focuses on Chandrayaan-2 XSM, Aditya-L1 HEL1OS, Solar Radio Bursts, Space Weather and 21-cm Neutral Hydrogen observations.

Current Research

Studying Quasi-Periodic Pulsations using HEL1OS onboard Aditya-L1, and investigating solar radio bursts observed with Chandrayaan-2 XSM.

Research Goal
