About

I am an Astronomy and Astrophysics researcher working in radio astronomy, solar physics, and RF instrumentation. My work focuses on solar radio bursts, flare energetics, and development of radio astronomy systems.


I developed a 3-meter automated radio telescope operating at 1.42 GHz for neutral hydrogen observations.

Projects

3m Automated Radio Telescope

Design and development of a fully automated 1.42 GHz radio telescope using SDR spectrometer.

Solar Radio Burst Analysis

Statistical analysis of Type II and Type III solar radio bursts and flare energetics.

Feedhorn & RF Systems

HFSS simulation and optimization of VE4MA feedhorns, choke rings, LNAs, and filters.
