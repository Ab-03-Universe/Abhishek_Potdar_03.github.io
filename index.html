Abhishek Potdar

Radio Astronomy • Solar Physics • RF Instrumentation

I am an Astronomy and Astrophysics researcher working in radio astronomy, solar physics, and radio instrumentation. My work focuses on solar radio bursts, flare energetics, radio telescope systems, and 21-cm neutral hydrogen observations. I am actively involved in development of radio astronomy instrumentation including feed systems, SDR-based receivers, signal processing systems, and automated radio telescope tracking.

Research

Solar Radio Bursts and Flares

Study of Type II and Type III radio bursts, flare energetics, and temporal association.

Current work involves statistical analysis of solar flare timing and radio burst association using multi-instrument observations.


Results include:
• Delay analysis
• Dynamic spectra
• Flare classification
• Radio/X-ray correlations

Radio Astronomy Instrumentation

Development of radio astronomy systems and RF instrumentation.

Development of feedhorns, SDR spectrometers, filters, low-noise amplifiers, and telescope automation systems.

21-cm HI Analysis

Neutral hydrogen observations at 1.42 GHz.

Analysis of galactic neutral hydrogen using automated radio telescope systems and SDR-based observations.

Radio Astrophysics and Imaging

Signal processing and imaging in radio astronomy.

Research involving radio signal analysis, astronomical imaging techniques, and spectroscopic observations.
