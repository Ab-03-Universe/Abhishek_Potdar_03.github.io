Solar Radio Physics • Radio Astronomy Instrumentation • Neutral Hydrogen Studies • Space Weather •

I am an astrophysics student learning radio astronomy instrumentation, solar radio physics and neutral hydrogen line studies. My work focuses on radio telescope development, antenna/feed design, RF systems, telescope automation, radio imaging and data analysis. I am particularly interested in low-frequency solar radio observations, solar physics and impact on space weather.
